# Harin (하린 · 夏凜) — TOPIK I (1급–2급) Korean Studio

**Harin (하린)** is the Korean TOPIK I sister platform to [Haruka](https://github.com/VonMAd/Haruka) — built as a 100% self-contained, single-file, zero-CDN web application with a unique **Goryeo Cheongja (Celadon Porcelain) & Dalbit Hanok (Moonlight Lacquer)** visual design, engineered to cover the complete official NIIED TOPIK I (1급 & 2급) syllabus.

## Live Studio
- **GitHub Pages:** https://vonmad.github.io/Harin/
- **Offline Single File:** Download [`Harin.html`](./Harin.html) and open it in any modern browser — no server, no build step, and no API key required.

## Complete Official TOPIK I Curriculum (9 Mastery Modules)
1. **Study Path & Smart SRS (`HarinSRS`)** — 5-Stage, 22-Milestone progressive Hangul → 1급 → 2급 roadmap with Leitner 5-Box spaced repetition, daily XP targets, and streak combos.
2. **Hangul Studio (한글)** — 86 Jamo, tense/aspirated consonants, compound vowels, and syllable blocks with a 4-quadrant Korean calligraphy writing pad and interactive quiz.
3. **Batchim & Sound Rules (받침)** — 86 final consonants, double batchim, and phonological rules (연음, 비음화, 구개음화, 경음화) with audio drills.
4. **Hanja Word Roots (한자어)** — 383 categorized Sino-Korean roots (`1급` & `2급`) with Eum-Hun readings, compound words, typing recall, and 3-mode quiz.
5. **Grammar Lab (문법)** — 177 verified official TOPIK I patterns (particles, tenses, irregulars, honorifics, connectors, promises `-(으)ㄹ게요`, written notices `-(으)ㅁ`, indirect speech, passive/causative) with usage notes, spoken examples, MC quiz, and Korean Sentence Builder.
6. **Vocabulary & Conjugation Studio (어휘)** — 1,762 official TOPIK I words (`813 1급` + `949 2급`) across 16 categories (including all interrogatives, demonstratives, discourse connectors, irregular verbs, honorifics, banking/airport/hospital/post traps, and mimetics) with 3D flashcards, **Korean Conjugation Studio (`해요체` / `합쇼체` + all irregular verb groups)**, Active/Passive pairs, typing drill, and 4-mode quiz.
7. **Reading Lab (읽기)** — 70 graded TOPIK I passages (`R01–R70`) covering **every official TOPIK I Reading format**: Topic Identification (Q31–33), Connector & Collocation Blank-Fill (Q34–39), Real Notices/Timetables/Receipts/Clinics/Posters (Q40–42), Essays & Cultural Stories (Q43–56), **Sentence Ordering `(가)(나)(다)(라)` (Q57–58)**, and **Sentence Insertion `(ㄱ)(ㄴ)(ㄷ)(ㄹ)` (Q59–70)**, with toggleable inline `{word|gloss}` study hints and sub-set pagination.
8. **Listening Lab (듣기)** — 80 graded TOPIK I listening scenarios (`L01–L80`) covering Best Response (Q1–4), Next Line (Q5–6), Place & Topic (Q7–14), Scene/Situation Match (Q15–16), Dialogue Detail & Central Thought (Q17–24), and Public Broadcasts & Long Dialogues (Q25–30), with sub-set pagination and full exam simulator.
9. **6-Mode Official TOPIK I Mock Exam Center (200-Point Timed Simulator)** — Full Official 70-Q Exam (`30 듣기 + 40 읽기`, 100 mins), TOPIK `2급` Challenge Exam (50 Qs), Quick 15-Q Sprint, Official 30-Q Listening-Only Exam (`100 pts`), Official 40-Q Reading-Only Exam (`100 pts`), and `1급` Foundation Exam (25 Qs), with official pass thresholds (`1급 ≥ 80/200`, `2급 ≥ 140/200`), mistake review, and auto-SRS scheduling.
